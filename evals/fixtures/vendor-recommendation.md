I wanted to reach out and follow up on the conversation we had a couple weeks

So I spent the last three weeks going through this. I started by pulling the

support team actually answers. The fourth one, Palletworks, never got back to me
after the second email, so I dropped them.

There's also the question of whether we do anything at all, since the current
system technically works, it's just painful. I did some rough math on the
overtime we're paying for the manual reconciliation and it's something like
$3,400 a month, plus Denise thinks we've lost maybe two people partly over this.

There are some risks. The Nimbus migration would need about six weeks of dual
running, and we'd need someone from IT dedicated to it for that period, which
Raj says is doable but tight. Also their contract has a 24 month minimum.

Anyway, on balance I think we should probably go with Nimbus, though I'm open to
discussing. Let me know when you have time to talk.
