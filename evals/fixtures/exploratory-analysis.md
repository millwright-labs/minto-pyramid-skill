# Churn poking-around, Q2

Pulled the Q2 cancellations to see if anything jumps out. Not sure it does yet.

Cancellations were 312, up from 280 in Q1, but Q1 had the holiday credit
promotion running through January so the base is odd. Adjusting for that,
roughly flat, maybe up slightly.

By plan: Starter churn is 4.1%, Pro is 2.2%, Enterprise is 0.8%. That ordering
is what you'd expect, and it hasn't moved much in four quarters.

By tenure: the spike is at month 2-3, which is also normal for us.

The support-ticket correlation is interesting — accounts that filed 3+ tickets
churned at 6.8% versus 2.4% baseline. But I can't tell direction from this.
Unhappy accounts file tickets, so the tickets might just be the symptom.

Two accounts we lost were large (Brightwell, Kanto Systems), and both cited
"consolidating vendors" which we can't do much about. If you exclude those two,
revenue churn looks fine and logo churn is the whole story.

I don't think I have enough here to say what's driving it, or whether anything
is. Might need the exit-survey text, which I haven't pulled.
